Origin Story

Why 'Burnpiles'?

My grandpa was a farmer with a burn pile — for brush, deadwood, and ideas. Sketches, half-plans, things he'd been carrying. When he was done with something, he'd walk it out to the pile.

He wasn't destroying them. He was releasing them. The signal had already been captured. Everything else was noise.

Burnpiles — burn the noise. Keep the signal.
